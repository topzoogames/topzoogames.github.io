How to play Zoo Kart

The kart drives forward by itself. On a computer, steer with the arrow keys (or A and D), brake with Down, and hold Space or Shift while turning to drift. On a phone, use the arrow buttons and hold DRIFT while turning. The longer you drift, the bigger the turbo when you let go. Drive over the orange arrow pads for a free boost.
