About Cheetah Drift Arena

Cheetah Drift Arena is a free driving game you can play right here in your browser. No download, no sign-up, no install. It works on a computer, phone or tablet.

A 3D drift arena under neon city lights. Throw your car sideways, chain huge slides and pull donuts round the cones.

Made by TopZoo Games.

How to play

On a computer, drive with the arrow keys or WASD and hold Space for the handbrake. On a phone the car speeds up by itself: hold the arrow buttons to steer and hold DRIFT to slide. Long, wide slides build your combo up to x5. Each session lasts 90 seconds; career mode has 30 levels.
